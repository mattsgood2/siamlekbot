BarberBot

Booking & walk-in queue for barbershopsDemo on request

Barbershops run on walk-ins and bookings at the same time, and most booking apps only handle one. BarberBot plans every chair across both, so the wait it shows customers is the real wait.

  • Online booking, with no app for customers to download
  • Live walk-in queue with honest wait times, on phones and a shop TV
  • SMS confirmations and reminders to cut no-shows
  • Pick-your-barber with each barber's own weekly schedule
  • Owner panel, deposits and subscriptions through Stripe
