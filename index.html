How it works

Hands-off, start to finish.

01

Guest messages you

A guest sends a WhatsApp to your number — from a QR code, your site, or your bio. 24/7, no staff needed.

02

The bot takes it

Date, party size, the details — and it answers questions about your menu, hours or rooms in natural language.

03

Confirm & remind

An instant confirmation, then a reminder before the booking. One tap to confirm or cancel — so no-shows drop.

04

Your team's in the loop

Staff get an instant alert on every booking, and everything's logged automatically in your own spreadsheet.
